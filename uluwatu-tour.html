About this tour

See the best of South Bali in a single unforgettable day. This private route takes you along dramatic clifftops, past turquoise water, and into the island's calm, sacred side — guided the whole way by Balinese locals who grew up here.

Discover hidden gems like Suluban Beach, reached through a narrow, cave-like path beneath the cliffs, and the quiet white sand of Tanah Barak Beach, where a winding walkway cuts between the rocks. End your day at Uluwatu Temple for one of the most beautiful sunsets in Bali, with the option to watch the traditional Kecak fire dance.

Finishing your Bali trip? This tour includes a private airport transfer, so you can enjoy your last moments on the island with no stress about getting to your flight.
