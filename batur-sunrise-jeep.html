About this tour

Watch the sunrise over Mount Batur, then ride a private 4WD Jeep off-road across the dramatic Black Lava field — with a professional photographer capturing every moment, and a relaxed stop at a Kintamani café overlooking the lake and mountains.

The route reaches a top private parking point for the best sunrise view over Mount Batur, before the Jeep adventure through the unique black lava terrain left by the volcano's past eruptions. A professional photographer and Korean-speaking guide are included — no extra charge.

Important to know

  • Groups of 1–2 use a comfortable City Car (5 seats); groups of 4–10 use a Hiace (14 seats).
  • Free pickup in the Ubud area. An extra Rp 50,000 applies for pickup in Seminyak, Kuta, Canggu, Jimbaran, or Nusa Dua.
  • Up to 2 pieces of luggage allowed in the City Car; more than 2 may need an extra vehicle (additional fee).
  • A child car seat is available on request for an extra USD 10.
  • For safety, pregnant guests of 5 months or more cannot join the tour.
  • Pickup time may shift slightly due to traffic — we confirm your pickup before departure.
