About this tour

The perfect mix of snorkeling and island sightseeing in one unforgettable day. Swim close to graceful manta rays at a world-famous snorkeling point, then tour Nusa Penida's most breathtaking cliffs and beaches — all with your own private group and Balinese local guides.

You'll snorkel several spots along the Nusa Penida coast, looking for manta rays, sea turtles, coral gardens and colourful fish, then head onto the island to see the iconic clifftop viewpoints up close.

Snorkeling spots

  • ✓Manta Bay — the famous manta ray point, Nusa Penida's best snorkeling spot
  • ✓Crystal Bay — very clear water, great for coral and marine life
  • ✓Gamat Bay — calm, clear water with coral and tropical fish
  • ✓Wall Bay — dramatic underwater cliff scenery

Island tour stops

  • ✓Kelingking Beach — the famous T-Rex shaped cliff and emerald water
  • ✓Angel's Billabong — a natural infinity rock pool
  • ✓Broken Beach — a round cliff with a natural stone arch over the sea
